## 주제

- 물을 많이 마시는 것의 장점
- 물을 많이 마시기 위해 내가 할 수 있는 것

## 영작

Since two months ago, I’ve started building a habit of drinking three cups of water every day. I hadn’t literally drunk a tiny cup of water a day. Also, since I liked having a meal with Cola, my body was often hydrated only by Cola. Even a year ago, I used to drink a regular cola, which contains a lot of sugar per a cup. I assume that my health might’ve been going worse if I kept living with the bad habit.

> About two months ago, I started getting into the habit of drinking three cups of water a day.
> 🤖: “Since two months ago” → since와 ago 는 같이 쓰지 않아요. since 는 지속, ago 는 과거의 시점
> “building a habit”은 문법상 맞지만 “building up a habit” 혹은 “getting into the habit”이 더 자연스러움.

> I used to drink barely even a small cup of water a day.
> I hardly drank any water at all.
> 🤖: Literally 는 감탄용으로는 괜찮지만 여기서는 어색함. 문맥상 used to 가 더 자연스러움

> I used to always have Coke with my meals, so my body was basicaly running on Coke instead of water.
> 🤖: having a meal with Cola → 너무 직역 느낌. I basically lived on Coke 가 더 자연스러움

> Even a year ago, I was still drinking regular Coke, which is loaded with sugar.
> 🤖: a regular Cola 보다 regular Coke 가 더 자연스러움. per a cup → per cup 혹은 per serving 이 맞음.

> I guess my health would’ve gotten worse if I had kept that bad habit.
> 🤖: going worse 는 어색함. getting worse 가 맞음.

Of course, I already knew that drinking Cola instead of water is a bad habit. Many youtube videos keep saying to reduce having sugar, hydrate myself often, and not grab a cup of coffee. Especially for me, coffee is the worst drink since I’ve been taking Concerta every day and it’s also a substance for stimulating my brain.

> Many Youtube videos keep telling people to cut down on sugar, stay hydrated, and avoid coffee.
> limit sugar consumption / cut down on sugar
> stay hydrated / drink enough water
> 🤖: “not grab a cup of coffee” → 너무 구체적. “avoid coffee”가 깔끔함. “reduce having”은 어색. “reduce sugar intake”가 정확함.

> Especially for me, coffee is the worst because I take Concerta every day, and caffeine overstimulates my brain.
> 🤖: “substance”는 약물 느낌이라 너무 과함. “for stimulating my brain” → 너무 직역투.

Anyways, I’ve drunk around three cups of water a day which is a good start for me. When I was 20 years old, I took a health class from the gym nearby my parents’ house. And my health trainer said me that I should drink 2L water every day! Obviously, I couldn’t believe the fact that I need to torture me with that much water. I actually tried to drink 2L, but drinking lots of water without no prepration was just painful to me. So after my private class was done, I stopped my life with water.

> Anyway, I’ve been drinking about three cups of water a day, which feels like a good start for me.
> 🤖: “Anyway”가 표준.

> My trainer told me I should drink about two liters of water every day!
> 🤖: “said me” ❌ → “told me”가 맞음.

> I couldn’t believe I had to torture myself with that much water.
> I couldn’t image forcing myself to drink that much.
> 🤖: “torture me” → “torture myself”, “believe the fact that” → “believe I needed to”로 단순화해야 자연스러움.

However, these days are a bit different from then. Firstly, I admitted that it’s not sustainable to begin with the goal in the sky. That’s why my daily goal is set to 3 cups of water. Also, I installed the app which I can manage the track of habits to make myself remember it. I’ve been using TickTick, and I’d like to recommend people who always forget a thing which you wanted to keep doing. It gives me a notification at 10:00, 13:00 and 18:00 which is the time I usually feel thirsty. Once you build a habit, it’ll not be a stressful thing since I’ve got familiar to that for a while.

> I also installed an app to help me keep track of my habits and remind me to drink water.
> 🤖: 문장 구조가 복잡하고 어색함. “manage the track of habits”는 번역투.
> keep track of habits / monitor habits / track progress
> remind myself / set reminders

> It notifies me at 10, 1, and 6 - right around the times I usually start feeling thirsty.
> 🤖: “which is the time” → 문법상 맞지만 너무 길고 딱딱함.

> Once you build the habit, it won’t feel stressful anymore since I’ve gotten used to it.
> 🤖: “I’ve got familiar to that” ❌ → “I’ve gotten used to it”. “it’ll not be”보단 “it won’t be”
## 알아보기
- say 는 말하다 (내용 중심), tell 은 말해주다 (대상 중심)
	- What was said vs Who was told
	- He said that he was tired → 단순 전달
	- He told me he was tired → 나에게 말했다는 점이 강조
	- He told me to go home → 명령/지시형
	- He said to go home → 구체적 대상 없이 일반적 진술
- drink / have water / stay hydrated / get enough water / take in fluids
- habit / routine / pattern / daily goal / practice
- bad habit / unhealthy routine / poor habit / not-so-good pattern
